Initial Situation & Challenge

In the fiercely competitive market for air passenger rights and EU-261 compensation, high CPCs and broad targeting waste quickly push acquisition costs into unprofitable territory — especially with "basket sizes" ranging from €250 claims up to €10,000 cases. Traditional campaigns often optimize purely for cheap clicks or form starts regardless of whether the claim is actually valuable and legally enforceable — which is why contribution-margin and ROAS optimization focused on lucrative cases is critical.

Results & Performance Leverage (Jan 1 – Dec 31, 2025)

  • ✓ Ad Spend Significantly Reduced: In full-year 2025, total ad costs dropped to €41,625.11 (a reduction of −€9,744.01 compared to the prior period), while simultaneously generating 7,924,088 impressions (+5,522,572 vs. prior period) and 1,226.17 conversions.
  • ✓ Conversion Value Massively Increased: Total conversion value rose to €208,176.40 (+€23,947.77 compared to the prior period).
  • ✓ Strong ROAS Driven by AI Bidding: Across the entire year, the Conversion Value to Cost ratio (ROAS) reached 5.00 (+1.41 vs. prior period) — meaning every €1.00 invested in ad spend generated an average of €5.00 in conversion value.

The Strategy

Instead of chasing raw traffic volume, the entire campaign and conversion architecture was re-engineered around value-based AI targeting (ROAS focus) and frictionless user guidance:

1. Value-Based AI Targeting (Value-Based Bidding)

AI-driven campaign models (including Performance Max and high-intent search campaigns) were trained specifically on actual conversion value rather than mere clicks. Budgets were systematically reallocated away from low-yield regions and concentrated on high-purchasing-power, high-converting audiences and flight routes.

2. Elimination of Funnel Friction

By analyzing real user sessions with Microsoft Clarity, drop-off bottlenecks within the multi-step claim eligibility check (especially on mobile devices) were identified and resolved.

3. Precise Signal Feedback Loop

Clean conversion tracking via Google Tags and privacy-compliant signal transmission fed real-time data back to the ad algorithms on which inquiries generated the highest contribution margin.

METHODOLOGY & TECHNOLOGY

Four Interlocking Gears for Maximum Campaign Profitability

Service 1

AI Targeting & ROAS-Driven Campaign Architecture

Modern ad platforms like Google Ads, YouTube, LinkedIn, and Meta operate with powerful AI bidding strategies — yet they are only as good as the data you feed them.

  • • Value-Based Bidding & Audience Signals: We align your campaigns not around cheap leads, but around real revenue and contribution margin values. Using first-party data, high-intent in-market signals, and precise negative lists, the AI pinpoints the exact decision-makers and customers with the highest deal value.
  • • Continuous Budget Allocation: Campaigns delivering above-average ROAS are systematically scaled, while budgets are immediately shifted if efficiency dips or audience saturation occurs.
  • • Multi-Channel Orchestration: Seamless integration of high-intent search campaigns (Google Search), data-driven Performance Max structures, B2B Account-Based Marketing (LinkedIn), and intelligent retargeting.
Service 2

Behavioral Analytics & UX Forensics with Microsoft Clarity

Buying clicks is easy — understanding why visitors fail to convert determines your profit. With Microsoft Clarity, we make the real behavior of your visitors across your website and forms visible.

  • • Heatmaps & Scroll-Depth Analysis: We see exactly which elements capture attention, where users scroll past critical value propositions, and which calls-to-action get ignored.
  • • Session Replays: Anonymized video recordings of real visits show us the exact moments where prospects hesitate, get confused, or abandon the funnel.
  • • Frustration Signal Detection: We systematically identify Rage Clicks (repeated rapid clicking on non-responsive elements), Dead Clicks (clicks on unlinked elements), and Quick Backs (immediate bounces), particularly on mobile devices.
Service 3

Conversion Process Optimization (CRO & Funnel Engineering)

Every unnecessary form field, ambiguous headline, or loading delay costs real money. We optimize the entire journey from the initial click to the submitted lead or completed checkout.

  • • Mobile-First Form & Checkout Optimization: With 65–70%+ of traffic often coming from mobile devices, we streamline input masks, integrate autocomplete features, and eliminate technical hurdles during document uploads or digital signatures.
  • • Landing Page Architecture & Message Match: We align ad copy and destination pages seamlessly, placing relevant trust signals (reviews, case studies, guarantees) right where the user makes a decision.
  • • Structured A/B Testing: Changes are never based on guesswork, but on data-backed hypotheses derived from Clarity and Analytics insights that are systematically tested against each other.
Service 4

End-to-End Attribution, Server-Side Tracking & CRM Integration

Without a clean data foundation, every AI campaign flies blind. We close the gap between the marketing click and actual closed revenue in sales.

  • • Server-Side Tracking & Enhanced Conversions: To compensate for data loss caused by cookie restrictions and ad blockers, we implement modern GDPR- and Swiss nDSG-compliant tracking including encrypted conversion signal transmission.
  • • Offline Conversion Import (CRM Feedback Loop): Especially in B2B, deals often close days or weeks after the initial phone contact. We feed qualified pipeline stages and real deal values from your CRM (e.g., Freshsales) back into Google & LinkedIn so the algorithm learns which clicks genuinely turn into paying customers.
  • • Transparent Live Dashboards: You can monitor in real time at any moment how ad spend, cost per qualified lead, conversion value, and ROAS are performing.
